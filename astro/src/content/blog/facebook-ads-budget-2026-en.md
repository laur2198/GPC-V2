## The question I get every week

"What's the starting budget for Facebook Ads?" Short answer: **minimum €30/day per ad set, minimum 3 ad sets active = €2,700 / month.**

The long answer depends on the vertical, what you sell and how competitive the auction is in your category.

## Why minimum budget went up

Three reasons:
1. **CPMs grew** 40-60% vs. 2022 in competitive categories.
2. **Learning phase** for Advantage+ Shopping requires ~50 events/week per ad set to exit.
3. **Creative diversification** is mandatory — a single hero creative with 3 variants no longer cuts it.

## Per-vertical recommendations (in 2026)

### DTC e-commerce

- **AOV < €50**: start at €90/day on Advantage+ Shopping, with mandatory CAPI server-side.
- **AOV €50-150**: €60/day is enough for initial testing; scale after creative validation.
- **AOV €150+**: €30-40/day is fine if you have a clear offer and mature retargeting.

### B2B lead-gen

- €40-60/day on native Lead Forms, mid-tier audiences (1-3% lookalikes from client list).
- Below €30/day: hard to optimize on lead quality, you need volume.

### Local services (medical, dental, beauty)

- €20-40/day on Lead Forms + strict geo targeting (15-20km radius around the location).
- Volume vs. quality — qualifying questions in the form mandatory.

## The learning phase trap

If you change creative, audience or budget by more than 20% on an active ad set, you reset it to zero. That's why you start with 2-3 ad sets identical in structure but with different creatives, and you keep the winner intact for 14 days.

## Conclusion

The right budget is the one that hits **the signal threshold** for the algorithm. Below it, you're in chaos. Above it, decisions get made on data.
