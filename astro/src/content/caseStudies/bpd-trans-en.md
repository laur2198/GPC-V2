## Context

BPD Trans is an intra-EU transporter from Brașov with fixed routes to Italy, Germany and France. Before the engagement they had an old WordPress site with Lighthouse 32, no proper tracking and no active acquisition channels.

## The challenge

Inbound lead volume was unpredictable — months with 0 contacts, other months with 30. Impossible to plan fleet capacity or scale operations.

## What we did

- **New site on Astro** — landing focused on routes and capacity, form with automatic qualification (route, frequency, cargo type).
- **Google Ads Search** — campaigns on geographic commercial intent ("transport Brașov–Italy", etc.) with route-specific landing pages.
- **Meta Ads** — B2B awareness targeting logistics managers in manufacturing and retail, with a lead magnet (transport cost calculator).
- **Server-side tracking** — GA4 with custom events, Meta CAPI for accurate attribution, Looker Studio dashboard with blended ROAS.

## Results at 6 months

Qualified lead volume grew from ~12/month to ~52/month, and average cost per lead dropped from €47 to €19. The sales team can now plan 60 days out, not just week-to-week.
