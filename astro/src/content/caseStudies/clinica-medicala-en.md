## Context

Private medical clinic in central Romania, with 6 specialists and a diversified offering (cardiology, endocrinology, dermatology). Strong local brand, but online pipeline was almost non-existent — only 8 new bookings per month from digital channels.

## The challenge

Marketing restrictions on medical categories (Meta forbids certain topics, Google has strict policies) had made previous campaigns impossible to scale without constant disapprovals. We needed a compliant but performant approach.

## What we did

- **Specialty-specific landing pages** — copy approved per medical advertising policies, social proof with real doctors, booking form integrated with the internal system.
- **Policy-compliant Meta Ads** — lead-gen campaigns with native Lead Forms (fewer rejections vs. external landing), creatives focused on trust and medical expertise.
- **Google Ads** — Search on symptom and specialist-name terms, with aggressive Negative Keywords to avoid sensitive queries.
- **Tracking** — server-side conversions, deduplication across platforms, dashboard with cost per booking by specialty.

## Results at 4 months

From 8 to 47 new bookings/month from digital channels. ROAS calculated on lifetime value (booking → treatment) reached 5.8x. The biggest win: predictability — the clinic can now allocate schedules 4 weeks in advance.
