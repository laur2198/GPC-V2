## Context

DTC fashion brand with 18 months of operation, ~€80k/month revenue. Working with a generalist agency on Meta Ads with ROAS oscillating between 1.8 and 2.5 — operating margin was fragile.

## The challenge

Both scaling and seasonality were issues. Above €8k/month on Meta budget, ROAS dropped below 2.0. And almost no valid signal on iOS due to incomplete tracking setup.

## What we did

- **Full audit** in the first 7 days: tracking, attribution, account structure, creative quality, site performance. Identified 11 friction points on checkout and 4 major leaks in setup.
- **Restructured Meta Ads** — campaign consolidation, Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns with weekly creative iteration (8 new angles/week), CAPI server-side via Stape.
- **Launched TikTok Ads** — Spark Ads with partnered creators, hooks tested with 4-6 variations, scaling on top performers.
- **Optimized Google Shopping** on the feed (titles, attributes, GTINs, images) and PMax with category-segmented Asset Groups.
- **CRO on the site** — A/B tests on PDP, simplified checkout (3 steps → 1 step), modular upsell that increased AOV.

## Results at 4 months

ROAS stabilized at 5.8x (blended view, including organic), budget grew from €8k to €28k/month with no efficiency loss. AOV climbed 24% thanks to checkout optimizations.
